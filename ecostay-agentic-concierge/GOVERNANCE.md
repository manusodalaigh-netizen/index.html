# EcoStay Ireland Governance Statement

## 1. Disclosure

Customers are told that the EcoStay Ireland Travel Concierge is AI-supported. The system explains that recommendations are generated using customer preferences, live package data, and live weather data. Customers are told that the recommendation is optional and does not confirm a booking.

## 2. Escalation

A human travel advisor should take over when:

- a package has zero availability;
- a price or availability value looks implausible;
- the customer asks for human help;
- the customer has accessibility, safety, dietary or health-related needs;
- the system cannot explain why it made a recommendation;
- the data source fails or returns incomplete information.

The named route is EcoStay customer support. The target response time is one business day.

## 3. Review cadence

The system should be reviewed monthly by the customer engagement owner. The review should check recommendation quality, live-data reliability, customer complaints, opt-out behaviour, availability errors, and whether the AI disclosure remains visible and understandable.

If the review identifies customer harm, misleading sustainability claims, repeated data errors, or unclear disclosure, the system should be changed or paused.
